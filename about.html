ABOUT RAILEASE

Travel planning,
made simpler.

RailEase – Your Journey, Simplified. RailEase is a smart and user-friendly train travel platform designed to make journey planning effortless and convenient. From exploring train routes and checking seat availability to booking tickets and managing travel details, RailEase brings everything together in one seamless experience. Our goal is to simplify railway travel through an intuitive, reliable, and hassle-free booking experience, making every journey smoother and more enjoyable.

Our idea

Bring route search, sample availability, passenger details and booking history into one clean experience.

Our promise

Be transparent: this prototype uses sample train data. It is not connected to Indian Railways and cannot issue real tickets.
